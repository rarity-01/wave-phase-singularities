# wave-phase-singularities
Independent theoretical preprint on wave-phase approaches to coordinate singularities of an event horizon


This paper resolves the mathematical and conceptual breakdown of time at the Schwarzschild limit by replacing traditional geometric coordinate infinities with a wave-mechanical threshold. Rather than relying on infinite time dilation or metric singularities, the model introduces a "phase-lock nullification" mechanism where temporal progression is reframed as an emergent property dependent on underlying wave coherence. By demonstrating that time terminates at the boundary due to a collapse in phase conditions rather than infinite spatial stretching, the framework successfully eliminates coordinate infinities and establishes a self-consistent, closed-loop theoretical alternative for boundary conditions where standard General Relativity breaks down.
